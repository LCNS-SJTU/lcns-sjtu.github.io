The Computational Neuroscience Lab is recruiting 2 postdoctoral researchers. The initial appointment will be for two years, with the possibility of renewal for a third year, and comes with a competitive annual salary. We are seeking highly motivated researchers to conduct high-quality interdisciplinary research, particularly in the following areas: **large-scale neural network modeling** and **brain-inspired AI algorithms**. This recruitment will remain open until all positions are filled.
Interested candidates should send the following application materials to <a href="mailto:zdz@sjtu.edu.cn">Douglas Zhou</a> and <a href="mailto:songting@sjtu.edu.cn">Songting Li</a>, with the email subject line: “Full Name + Postdoctoral Application”.


Application materials:

•	Curriculum vitae;

•	Full publication list and PDFs of representative papers;

•	After an initial screening, two recommendation letters will be requested.
